# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in this RF security research project. Contributions that improve the **educational and defensive** value of this work are welcome.

## Before you contribute

By opening an issue or pull request, you agree that your contribution:

1. Is intended for **educational / defensive-security** purposes, consistent with the [DISCLAIMER](DISCLAIMER.md).
2. Does **not** add weaponised, ready-to-run attack tooling, exploit payloads, or anything whose primary purpose is to harm or illegally interfere with systems or spectrum you do not own.
3. Respects all applicable laws and responsible-disclosure norms.

## What makes a good contribution

- ✅ Clearer explanations, corrections, or additional background in the `docs/`.
- ✅ Defensive analysis, detection ideas, and mitigation guidance.
- ✅ Better diagrams, references, or reproducibility notes (at a conceptual level).
- ✅ Fixes to typos, links, formatting, or structure.

## What will be rejected

- ❌ Turnkey jamming/spoofing tools or scripts targeting specific products.
- ❌ Captured signals/keys intended to control third-party devices.
- ❌ Anything encouraging illegal or unauthorized activity.

## How to submit

1. Fork the repository and create a descriptive branch.
2. Keep changes focused and documented.
3. Open a pull request explaining **what** you changed and **why**, and confirm it complies with the points above.

## Reporting issues

Use GitHub Issues for documentation errors, broken links, or suggestions. Please do **not** post content that would facilitate misuse.

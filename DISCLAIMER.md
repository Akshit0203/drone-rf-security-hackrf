## The law

Radio spectrum is regulated. In most jurisdictions it is a **criminal offence** to:

- **Jam or intentionally interfere** with radio communications (e.g. in the US this violates the *Communications Act of 1934*; the FCC prohibits the sale, use, or marketing of jammers — comparable laws exist in the EU, UK, India, and most countries).
- **Intercept or decode** communications you are not a party to or authorized to receive.
- **Transmit** on licensed/regulated frequencies or above permitted power without authorization.
- **Take control of or interfere with an aircraft**, including unmanned aircraft — often an aggravated offence.

Drones are also subject to **aviation regulations** (FAA, EASA, DGCA, etc.), including Remote ID and no-fly-zone rules.
